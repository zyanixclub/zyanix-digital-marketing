The Initiative

More than a hub. A creative community.

ZYANIX Digital Media Marketing Hub is being introduced as a platform for students to discover, learn and experiment with digital media.

The inauguration brings together technology, creativity and practical experience through an expert webinar and a hands-on creative challenge.
